Install Label

On iPhone and iPad, Label runs from the Home Screen so printing, saving and offline access all work properly.

  1. 1Tap the Share button in Safari — the square with an arrow.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add, then open Label from your Home Screen.
